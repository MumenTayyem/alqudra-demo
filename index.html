FAQ

Questions we hear every day

Can I do everything online without coming to the shop?

Yes. Send clear photos or PDFs of your documents on WhatsApp to 050 827 1036. We type, submit and pay the government fee, you pay us online, and the processed documents come back to you on WhatsApp and email.

What documents do I need for a family visa renewal?

Sponsor's passport and Emirates ID, the dependent's passport and current visa, a tenancy contract or Tawtheeq, a recent salary certificate or labour contract, and a passport-size photo. We will tell you if anything else applies to your case.

How long does a Mulkiya or driving licence renewal take?

Usually the same day, once the vehicle passing test or eye test is done. We book the test appointment for you and type the renewal as soon as the result is in.

Do you print from WhatsApp or email?

Yes. Send the file to 050 827 1036 or alqudra.alain@gmail.com with the number of copies and whether you want colour. Your prints are ready when you arrive.

Do you do hard binding for theses and project reports?

Yes. Spiral, soft and hard binding with gold-embossed titles are done in store. Hard binding is ready within 24 hours.

Are your legal translations accepted by government offices?

Yes. Translations are done by a Ministry of Justice certified translator and are accepted by courts, immigration and all UAE government departments.
